Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about how your visit pass works, privacy protection, and travel calculations.

1. How does QueueLess calculate my travel time without tracking me?
QueueLess uses a one-time mathematical distance calculation (Haversine formula) in your browser. It asks for your location once only to evaluate distance in kilometers and calculate transit minutes (Walk: 10–12m/km, 2-Wheeler: 1.5–2m/km, Car: 1.5–2m/km). Your GPS location is never logged, stored on any server, or shared.
2. What happens when I apply for an appointment pass?
When you submit an appointment request, you instantly receive a digital visit pass with a verified QR code in Pending Approval status. The organization administrator or doctor reviews the request. If approved, it is pushed directly into the assigned staff's To-Do list. If declined, your screen immediately displays a clear cancellation alert with the doctor's reason.
3. How is staff personal privacy protected from other staff members?
Under our strict Role-Based Access Control (RBAC), when a staff member logs in, they see their own full details (Staff ID, contact phone, residential address, department). For all colleagues, they only see Name and Designation. Sensitive phone numbers and home addresses of peers are strictly masked.
4. Can a client access internal staff or admin dashboards?
No. When signed in as a Client, the navigation bar shows only client-relevant links. Furthermore, strict browser page guards automatically block clients from loading admin or staff portals, redirecting them safely back to their appointment portal.
5. How can I run QueueLess on Windows, Android, Mac, or iOS?
QueueLess provides standalone packages for every platform: Launch-QueueLess-Windows.bat on Windows, QueueLess.apk for Android, QueueLess.dmg for Mac, and QueueLess.ipa for iOS. The built-in Java server automatically broadcasts on your local Wi-Fi.
